# Word Practice

A small app for practising English words. Brett made it for GEAP 103.

## Add a word to Brett's app

Use a computer, not your phone.

1. You need to be in our class group, GEAP-103. If you can see this page, you're in.
2. Open https://github.com/settings/emails and tick **Keep my email addresses private**. Then your commit won't show your email address.
3. Click **Fork**, then **Create fork**. Now you have your own copy, a fork.
4. Choose one English word that a beginner needs (not *library*). In your fork, click **+** (or **Add file**), then **Create new file**. Name it `words/`, then your word, then `.md`, in small letters: for example, `words/library.md`.
5. Copy this pattern, with your word:

   ```
   # your word
   Meaning: what it means
   Example: a sentence with your word
   ```

   Click **Commit changes...**, then **Commit changes**.
6. Click **word-practice-app** at the top. Click **Contribute**, then **Open pull request**. Write a title and one sentence. Click **Create pull request**.

The same steps, with pictures, are on slides 5 to 8: https://brettrey.github.io/geap-103/materials/pull-requests-projector.html#5
